# Experimental live score prediction

The live headline predicts the measured total at 3,600 active seconds. The card shows only the prediction label and number. Earned points and training details are in the help tooltip. A small target at the 60-minute edge of the points-over-time chart marks the estimate; hover or focus it for the numeric value. Out-of-scale estimates use an edge indicator. At the deadline the measured score takes over. Predictions never change reports, rankings, execution order, model settings or stopping behavior.

For each prefix length n, fit a separate linear ridge regression with an unpenalized intercept. Inputs are the n signed attempt scores divided by two, followed by active elapsed time divided by 3,600. The target is the recorded final one-hour score. Ridge alpha is 0.1. A centered dual solve keeps fitting inexpensive when saved runs are few; the currently needed prefix is fitted on demand from the loaded run history, without a new service or dependency.

Training uses each eligible full-hour run once, at the completion time of its nth resolved attempt. Correct answers contribute their frozen weight, incorrect answers minus one, and abstentions, unsupported-vision results and timeouts zero. Execution errors and synthetic unattempted/stopped records are not completed attempts. Duplicate persisted attempts are removed; distinct repeat-round attempts remain separate. Active intervals exclude pauses.

Eligibility requires matching benchmark version, scoring policy, round policy, timeout policy, frozen question-bundle hashes, weights and expected order. The observed prefix must contain the same task/round sequence. Training runs must reach the full hour, have a final measured score, and have loaded attempt contributions that reproduce that score. Partial runs, the current run, repair composites and invalidated timing/results are excluded. Archived runs remain eligible when present in the loaded history. There must be at least two eligible examples for the current prefix; otherwise the UI displays earned points and explains that the forecast is waiting for data. There is no silent straight-line fallback.

The result is explicitly experimental, with the number of training runs and resolved attempts available in the help tooltip. No prediction interval, accuracy guarantee or automatic triage is claimed. Few examples, correlated runs, identical early outcomes, elapsed-time extrapolation during long questions, and selecting training runs that reached the prefix all limit interpretation. Closing or refreshing the page refits from saved evidence; no trained artifacts or private run data are published.
